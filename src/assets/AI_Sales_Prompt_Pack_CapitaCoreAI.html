📖 How to Use This Prompt Pack

  1. Browse All Prompts: Start in the "Browse Prompts" tab to explore all 55 prompts organized by sales stage.
  2. Filter by Category: Use the category pills to focus on prompts relevant to your current sales challenge.
  3. Search: Use the search bar to find prompts by keyword (e.g., "discovery", "pricing", "objection").
  4. Copy & Use: Click the "Copy" button on any prompt to copy it to your clipboard, then paste it into your AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.).
  5. Save Favorites: Click the star icon to save prompts you use frequently. Access them in the "Favorites" tab.
  6. Customize: Add your own custom prompts in "My Custom Prompts" tab and build your personal playbook.

⚡ Pro Tips for Maximum ROI

  1. Personalize Every Prompt: Replace placeholder tags like [COMPANY NAME], [INDUSTRY], [PAIN POINT] with real details. Generic = ignored.
  2. Add Context: The more specific context you provide to your AI, the better the output. Don't be shy with details.
  3. Iterate: If the first output isn't quite right, refine your prompt and try again. AI gets smarter with feedback.
  4. Build Your Stack: Use these prompts in combination. E.g., Prospect Research + Company Deep Dive → Cold Email → Follow-up Sequence.
  5. Share with Your Team: Export your favorites as a team playbook. Consistency in messaging drives better results.
  6. Track What Works: Note which prompts and angles generate the highest reply rates and discovery conversion. Double down on winners.
  7. Update Seasonally: Industry trends change. Revisit your prompts quarterly and add custom variations for new markets or personas.

💡 Best Practices for Sales Prompt Engineering

  • Be Specific: "Write an email to a CTO about API integration" is better than "write a sales email".
  • Add Constraints: Tell the AI the tone, length, format you want. E.g., "Under 100 words, peer-to-peer tone, no sales jargon".
  • Use Examples: Include 1-2 examples of what good looks like, and the AI will match the style.
  • Test & Measure: A/B test prompt variations. Track open rates, reply rates, meeting booked rates.
  • Iterate on Feedback: If a prospect replies "too salesy", adjust your prompt to be more consultative and test again.
